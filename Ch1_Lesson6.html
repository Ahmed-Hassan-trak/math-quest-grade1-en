1-6 Let's Count! (From 6 to 10) 🔢

Learn the Japanese Math Method: Point at each picture with your finger as you count out loud!

📖 Point & Learn (نشير ونتعلم)

When we count, we point to each picture with our finger and say the numbers out loud!

Example: Tap each bird to point and count out loud! 🕊️
Tap the birds one by one!
👈
Japanese Method Rule: Always touch or point to each item once so you don't miss any or count it twice!
